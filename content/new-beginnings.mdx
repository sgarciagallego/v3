Hey there! Are you new here? Oh! So am I! My name is Sergio. Welcome to my new digital home. After a few years at my beloved [v2](https://v2.sergiogarciagallego.com), I felt it was time for a change. I decided to relocate to [v3](https://v3.sergiogarciagallego.com) but did not feel quite at home, so after a few months, I decided to move out and come here! I hope you are as cosy as I am.

Once you're ready, let me show you around...

If you’ve been to my previous homes, you’ll recognise the same furniture: there’s the chair of injection-moulded stress, and there’s the dining table made from reclaimed creativity. That is, of course, the same lamp. Yes, 60-watt optimism. Oh, and let’s not forget the sofa I got off Facebook Marketplace, yes it’s second-hand, but treated with love and care.

---

This is the 4<sup>th</sup> iteration of my personal website, where I have introduced a few new rooms: my [first-ever portfolio](/portfolio), and [blog](/blog). You might be thinking: [why password-protect my portfolio?](https://www.reddit.com/r/graphic_design/comments/4kdr3h/password_protected_portfolios_yay_or_nay/) And that’s okay, I need to keep some case studies hidden as they haven’t reached the public domain yet.

Here are some technologies I’ve made good use of here:

- [Next.js 14 (App Router)](https://nextjs.org/blog/next-14) because why not, it’s built on React and server components are great.
- [CSS modules](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/building-your-application/styling/css-modules#css-modules) to keep my application as lightweight as possible.
- [NextAuth.js](https://next-auth.js.org) to password-protect my [portfolio](/portfolio) route.
- [next-mdx-remote](https://github.com/hashicorp/next-mdx-remote) to keep my focus on writing when blogging.
- [sugar-high](https://sugar-high.vercel.app) used to syntax highlight my more technical blogs and case studies.

As well as this, you might also notice native theme handling at the system-level, to keep that minimal feel, whilst still following your preferences. On a less technical but more important note, I'm committed to ensuring my builds are [accessible](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). As a result, I welcome any [feedback](mailto:gallegogarciasergio@gmail.com) that highlights areas where my work may fall short.

Thanks for stopping by! Whether you're here for the first time or returning from a previous version, I’m thrilled to have you. Sit back, relax, and enjoy what I bring here in the future!
